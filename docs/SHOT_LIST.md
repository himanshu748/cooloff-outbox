# Cool-off Outbox — 60-second live-webcam take

For Himanshu's own webcam and voice. These are **edited-film timecodes**: allow
real calibration and holds to finish, then cut waiting with a visible time-jump
caption. Never speed the app's clock, inject values or pass off the fake-camera
development check as live input. Record voice separately if speaking hurts
signal confidence. No physiological change is guaranteed; rehearse honestly.

## Setup

- Even front-facing light, a steady camera and face **and upper chest** in frame.
  Sit comfortably, stay reasonably still and breathe naturally. No breath holding
  or forced exertion to raise heat. The text rules can create the heated example.
- [Try it: downloadable builds](../README.md#try-it--no-cloning-needed), or run
  `npm ci` and `npm start`. Obtain a free Presage key via the README. For the
  recording, supply `SMARTSPECTRA_API_KEY` through your environment privately;
  outside recording, the in-app password field works too and is not saved to
  disk. Never display, echo, record or commit the key. Leave
  `COOLOFF_FAKE_CAMERA` unset. Confirm full metric authorization beforehand.
- OBS Studio: capture only the app window; hide terminals, notifications and
  private paths. Test voice levels. Keep labels/readings legible and captions
  below them. Use a dummy recipient and harmless original sample messages.
- Leave telemetry off. Start with a fresh consent-first take. Rehearse full
  readiness and at least 20 seconds of confident pulse and breathing. Let the
  baseline actually finish; never label a missing estimate as zero or calm.
- Have a mail app ready with a harmless test recipient. Do not actually deliver
  to anyone. Explicitly disclose that mailto opens a draft, not an automatic send.

Rehearse **Try a heated example**: it fills all three fields and asks before
overwriting a non-empty composer. Keep the **Why** bar and evidence lines
legible, then the card's captured **Held because**. The staged text alone scores
45/100, above the hold threshold of 40; there is no need to manufacture a change
in camera estimates. For the calm-send shot, wait for genuinely below-threshold
heat instead of changing readings.

Alternative unlock take: demonstrate **Use without camera** and label its honest
30-second timer-only unlock. Do not describe that as a measured cooldown. The
all-caps/hostile text itself can keep heat above threshold even if vitals fall;
do not promise that breathing alone will change the result.

The receipt includes pulse/breathing only when confident samples exist. In
timer-only mode it shows just time and text heat; absent physiological data
must not be narrated as a fall in pulse or breathing. A flat sparkline is an
honest result. Do not replace it with a more dramatic curve.

Watch the final take with sound. Check real signals, visible confidence, the
correct unlock reason, no key/personal content, and no actual outgoing email.
