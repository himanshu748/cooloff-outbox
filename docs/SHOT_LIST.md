# Cool-off Outbox — 60-second live-webcam take

For Himanshu's own webcam and voice. These are **edited-film timecodes**: allow
real calibration and holds to finish, then cut waiting with a visible time-jump
caption. Never speed the app's clock, inject values or pass off the fake-camera
development check as live input. Record voice separately if speaking hurts
signal confidence. No physiological change is guaranteed; rehearse honestly.

## Setup

- Even front-facing light, a steady camera and face **and upper chest** in frame.
  Sit comfortably, stay reasonably still and breathe naturally. No breath holding
  or forced exertion to raise heat. The text rules can create the heated example.
- `npm ci`, supply `SMARTSPECTRA_API_KEY` through your shell environment privately,
  then `npm start`. Never display, echo, record or commit the key. Leave
  `COOLOFF_FAKE_CAMERA` unset. Confirm full metric authorization beforehand.
- OBS Studio: capture only the app window; hide terminals, notifications and
  private paths. Test voice levels. Keep labels/readings legible and captions
  below them. Use a dummy recipient and harmless original sample messages.
- Leave telemetry off. Start with a fresh consent-first take. Rehearse full
  readiness and at least 20 seconds of confident pulse and breathing. Let the
  baseline actually finish; never label a missing estimate as zero or calm.
- Have a mail app ready with a harmless test recipient. Do not actually deliver
  to anyone. Explicitly disclose that mailto opens a draft, not an automatic send.
