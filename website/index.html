Drafts stay in memory.

No accounts or draft autosave. Close the app and your messages and vitals are gone. An optional local counter stores totals only.

02

Your camera. Your call.

Off until you opt in and prepare it. Frames and vitals are processed on-device, not uploaded. No microphone, ever.

03

A cue. Not a diagnosis.

Camera estimates are not medical and cannot tell how you feel. Heat is a fallible writing cue, never a verdict.

Optional camera mode uses Presage SmartSpectra. Authentication and model downloads need a network connection. SDK diagnostics are opt-in and off by default.

A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Room for
the details.

Do I have to turn on my camera?

Not at all. Timer-only mode needs no API key or camera. Heated drafts pause for 30 seconds. Camera mode is a separate, explicit choice and uses your own free Presage developer key.

What happens during a cooling hold?

You can edit or delete anytime, and a confirmed override becomes available after 10 seconds. With a confident camera baseline, a hold unlocks after 30 continuous seconds below the heat threshold, or after 3 minutes at most. Nothing sends when the timer finishes.

Does it send emails for me?

No. It opens a prefilled draft in your default mail app, where you still choose Send. You can also copy your message into another app. Heated messages use the same cooling hold for both actions.

What can the camera actually tell?

SmartSpectra estimates pulse, breathing, and expressions when it has enough confidence. The app compares available signals with your personal baseline. It cannot know your feelings or intentions, and missing readings are never invented.
